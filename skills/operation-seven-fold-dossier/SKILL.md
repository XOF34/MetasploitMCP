# Operation Seven-Fold Dossier

Use this framework as a case-management checklist for Texas motor-vehicle personal-injury matters. It is an organizational aid, not legal advice; verify current law, deadlines, court rules, and local requirements with qualified counsel.

## Operating phases

1. **FRONT GATE — Intake and engagement:** collect accident, party, insurance, vehicle, employment, and pre-existing-condition information; execute the engagement agreement, HIPAA authorizations, wage verification, property-damage authorization, tax authorization, and any limited case-specific power of attorney; calculate and calendar limitations.
2. **FIELD GLASS — Investigation:** request the crash report and signal data; identify and preserve surveillance; interview witnesses; document the scene and vehicles; send targeted preservation/spoliation notices.
3. **BODY COUNT — Medical documentation and damages:** obtain complete records, imaging, bills, and lien information; maintain a treatment chronology; document wage loss; obtain causation/prognosis narratives; prepare life-care and future-loss analyses when appropriate; assess maximum medical improvement.
4. **PRESSURE POINT — Demand and settlement:** prepare a fact-supported demand with liability, treatment, economic and non-economic damages, attachments, response deadline, and policy-limit analysis; review releases, liens, minor approvals, and structured-settlement terms before execution.
5. **OPEN FILE — Litigation:** file the petition and case information sheet; serve process; manage disclosures, interrogatories, document requests, admissions, subpoenas, depositions, expert designations, meet-and-confer efforts, motions to compel, protective orders, and discovery deadlines.
6. **VERDICT LINE — Trial preparation:** comply with the docket-control order; prepare the trial notebook, witness and exhibit lists, motions in limine, proposed jury charge, voir-dire questions, examination outlines, opening and closing arguments, and verdict form.
7. **CLEAN SWEEP — Judgment enforcement:** secure the signed judgment and bill of costs; record abstracts; investigate assets; use post-judgment discovery and execution tools where appropriate; confirm payment before filing satisfaction and releases.

## Reserve assets

Evaluate case-specific needs for a health-care-claim notice, dram-shop notice, UM/UIM claim, Medicare conditional-payment request, hospital or medical lien notice, affidavit of no insurance, certified-mail proof, and periodic client status reports.

## Execution rules

- Start preservation, crash-report, and surveillance work immediately after retention.
- Treat every deadline as jurisdiction- and order-dependent; calendar the governing source rather than relying on this checklist alone.
- Keep an auditable case file containing signed forms, delivery proof, records, bills, liens, correspondence, deadlines, and status updates.
- Do not sign a broad release, settle a minor’s claim, or disburse proceeds until authority, lien resolution, and required approvals are confirmed.
- Adapt the phase checklist to the facts; cases may resolve before litigation or require deployment through enforcement.
